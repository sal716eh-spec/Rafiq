# Brag Plan: Rafiq (رَفِيق)

## What is this app?
Rafiq teaches Modern Standard Arabic in short, fully vowelled daily steps: you meet words, check yourself straight away, then hold a real conversation in your own words and get told at once whether your reply fits.

## The angle
A quiet premium product film. No hype: the Arabic script itself is the hero, and the product is shown doing the two things that make it different — meeting a word and checking it, then replying in your own words and being understood. The film is built from the app's own paper-and-ink palette, its Arabic hero line, and the real lesson and conversation screens.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The word **رَفِيق** (Rafiq, "companion") writes itself across a warm paper frame at full scale, right to left, in the app's ink colour. Below it, a small monospace label: RAFIQ · YOUR ARABIC COMPANION. Then the site's headline settles: "Learn to read, understand and speak Arabic".

## Key moments (the middle)
- A New words card: **صَدِيق** · ṣadīq · friend — then the app's "What does this mean?" check: four answers arrive, "friend" is chosen and turns green with a ✓.
- The conversation partner: a neighbour says "السَّلامُ عَلَيْكُمْ. أَنا جارُكَ الْجَدِيدُ." — the learner types their own reply "وَعَلَيْكُمُ السَّلامُ، أَهْلًا وَسَهْلًا!" — and the app answers "✓ Good reply."

## Outro / punchline
The Arabic hero line from the site, **رَفِيقُكَ فِي تَعَلُّمِ الْعَرَبِيَّةِ** (your companion in learning Arabic), with الْعَرَبِيَّةِ in the rubric red. The logo tile and "Rafiq" land together; rafiq-arabic.com beneath. Music fades. Silence.

## User flow worth showing
Continue a lesson → meet a word and check it → reply in a conversation and get instant feedback. (Scenes 2 and 3 are the centerpiece and show this flow, recreated from learn.html.)

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film — manuscript paper, ink and one red accent
- Interpretation: four scenes, long settled holds, slow crossfades (0.7s), mixed-case type, no lists, no claims beyond what the app does.

## Duration: 20.5 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (steady and clean), trimmed to 21s
- Music treatment: fade in over 1s, bed at ~0.3, fade out from 18.6s to the end under the logo
- Music cue guidance: preset cues/…vol-12….music-cues.md, 109.96 BPM. Strong cues used: 8.74s (✓ on "friend"), 13.11s (✓ Good reply), 17.47s (logo lands). Beat grid for the four answer options: 6.00, 6.56, 7.09, 7.64 (short labels, then held together until 9s).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — music RMS gently swells the warm background glow and the logo tile's shadow; no waveforms.
- SFX posture: minimal — soft bong on each ✓, one quiet click on "send", one soft impact under the logo.
- Audio-coupled moments: answer chosen, reply sent, reply approved, logo landing.
- Restraint rule: no typing clicks per character, nothing bright or repeated.
