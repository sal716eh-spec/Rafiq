# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Rafiq v7 (everything inside)

## Objective
A guided tour of every Rafiq feature, in the format of the website's current video (brag-output-v6).

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output-v7/composition/` (index.html generated by `brag-output-v7/src/build.py` from `src/index.tpl.html`; scene timing follows the recorded voice)
- Rendered video: `brag-output-v7/brag.mp4`, poster `brag.jpg`, captions `brag.en.vtt`
- Format: landscape 1920x1080, 30 fps · Duration: about 79s

## Source Material
- Files read: index.html (landing copy: units, features, plans), dashboard.html (Today card), practise.html (Practise menu), session.html + tiles.js (word tiles), toolkit-data.js (verb ذَهَبَ, the ثُمَّ exercise), scenes-data.js (six scenes), spelling.js, brag-output-v6 (chat and weak-spots scenes, palette, sound)
- Copy that must appear verbatim: Continue · Reading Arabic · the 12 unit names · Meet / Listen / Understand / Practise / Speak · Weak-spots review · Spelling bee · Beyond the lessons · Real-life scenes · Redo a unit · Complete · ✓ Good reply. · ✓ Good reply — but check this: · rafiq-arabic.com · Start your first lesson free

## Creative Direction
- Tone: polished; a guided tour inside v6's manuscript look
- Hook: ink stroke → Today card → Continue pressed
- Outro: "One path to follow, and practice whenever you want more. A few minutes a day." → brand → "Start your first lesson free at rafiq-arabic.com."
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, invented numbers, features that aren't live (the salah track)

## Visual Identity
As v6/v8 (paper, card, ink, red, verdigris, gold; Karla, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, JetBrains Mono; frame rules, meta labels, ghost ع, warm glow).

## Storyboard
See `brag-plan.md` (14 scenes, voice-timed).

## Audio
Natural bed (fountain, birdsong, room tone) under the narrator and Rafiq's voice; native clips from the app; taps, pen, bong, page turns (v6 set); glow follows the voice RMS.

## Hyperframes Instructions
hyperframes-core contract (one paused timeline on `window.__timelines["main"]`, timed clips, audio ids, local fonts), hyperframes-animation (opacity/transform), `check` as the gate, then `render --quality high`.
