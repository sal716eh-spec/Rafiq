How it works

Every word and sentence you answer is scheduled to come back at a widening interval — a day, then a few days, then a week, then longer. Get something wrong and it returns sooner. This is spaced repetition, and it is the most reliable way we know of moving vocabulary into long-term memory. Your daily load is capped so you are never faced with a wall of reviews, and the number of new words a day adjusts to the level you choose when you sign up.

Rafiq is a companion, not a replacement. It doesn't teach Arabic from scratch or follow any one curriculum — it gives you a faster way to drill the vocabulary and sentences you're already learning, whoever you're learning them from. Vowel marks and model answers are a study aid — worth checking against your teacher, especially case endings.

Getting started

Create an account and you will be asked three quick questions about your level so the pace suits you. Your progress saves to your account, so you can study on your phone and pick up on your laptop.
