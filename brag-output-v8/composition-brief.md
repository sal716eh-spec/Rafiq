# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Rafiq v8 (the story)

## Objective
A story film for Rafiq in the format of the website's current video (brag-output-v6): why it exists, the memory research it is built on, and how it starts a complete beginner.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output-v8/composition/` (index.html is generated by `brag-output-v8/src/build.py` from `src/index.tpl.html`, so scene timing follows the recorded voice)
- Rendered video: `brag-output-v8/brag.mp4`, poster `brag.jpg`, captions `brag.en.vtt`
- Format: landscape 1920x1080, 30 fps
- Duration: about 80s (voice-led)

## Source Material
- Project root: /home/user/Rafiq
- Primary files read: index.html (landing copy), path.js (STREAK_GOALS research lines and sources), alphabet-data.js (letters, examples, listening test), learn.html (reading starter screens), brag-output-v6 (format, palette, sound design)
- Product name: Rafiq · رَفِيق
- Strongest claim: "Everything you learn comes back for review just before you'd forget it."
- UI to recreate: the reading starter's alphabet chart, the ب letter card with red letters, the listening test, a review quiz card
- Copy that must appear verbatim: "Keeping them is the hard part." · the three sources (Cepeda et al., 2006 · Roediger & Karpicke, 2006 · Lally et al., 2010) · rafiq-arabic.com · Try it free

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: polished; direction: the quiet manuscript film of v6, told as the story behind the name
- Hook: ink stroke, رَفِيق written right to left, Rafiq's voice: "رَفِيق. In Arabic, it means companion."
- Outro: "A few minutes a day, with a companion beside you." → brand → "Try رَفِيق free today."
- Avoid: generic SaaS language, invented history or numbers, abstract filler

## Visual Identity
Paper #f1ece0, card #f7f3ea, ink #17262b, ink soft #4f6163, red #b4322a, verdigris #2e7263, gold #a8842c; Karla, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, JetBrains Mono (local woff2); frame rules, meta labels, the faint ghost ع and warm glow from v6.

## Storyboard
See `brag-plan.md` (13 scenes, voice-timed).

## Audio
- Role: natural-sound bed (fountain, birdsong) under two voices; no music (as v4-v6)
- Voices at -16 LUFS; native Arabic clips loudness-matched; background about 18 dB under
- SFX: v6's generated set (opener, pen stroke/write, page turn, tap, swell) plus soft UI accents (bong, impactSoft, paper)
- Audio-reactive: the background glow follows the RMS of the voice track (subtle)

## Hyperframes Instructions
Follow hyperframes-core (one paused GSAP timeline registered on `window.__timelines["main"]`, clips with data-start/duration, every <audio> with an id, local fonts with @font-face), hyperframes-animation (opacity/transform tweens, crossfades), and hyperframes-cli (`check` as the gate, then `render`). Keep all text readable; hold sequential text on screen.
