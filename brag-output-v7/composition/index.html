Weak-spots review · came up 3 times this week
Masculine and feminine must match

Words that describe or point to a noun take its gender, and feminine nouns usually end in ة.

هَذا بَيْتٌ كَبِيرٌThis is a big house
هَذِهِ غُرْفَةٌ كَبِيرَةٌThis is a big room
