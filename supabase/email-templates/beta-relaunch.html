Thank you for being one of Rafiq’s first testers. Your early feedback shaped a lot of what we’ve built since.

Rafiq is very different now

We’ve rebuilt most of it:

  • A guided path, one step at a time, with native Arabic audio
  • Reviews that bring words back before you forget them
  • Word tiles, a spelling bee and real-life conversations
  • Everyday essentials: numbers, days, months, colours and telling the time
  • A built-in Arabic keyboard with vowel marks
  • A short video showing how it all works

We’ve reset everyone’s progress

Because so much has changed, we’d like you to start again from the beginning and see Rafiq the way a new learner will. When you sign in, you’ll answer a few quick questions, watch the tour and begin your journey.

You may need a new password

Passwords now need at least 8 characters. If yours is shorter, you’ll be asked to choose a new one when you sign in.

We’d love your honest feedback

What’s confusing, what’s broken, what you like and what you’d change. Please email us at feedback@rafiq-arabic.com. Screenshots help a lot.

We launch on 10 October

Rafiq goes live on 10 October, in shā’ Allāh. What you tell us now goes straight into the launch version.

What we’re working on next

  • Your salah: understand every word you say in your prayer, word by word, and connect it to the Arabic you’re learning
  • Speaking practice: say your replies out loud and get feedback
  • Daily reminders to help you keep your streak
  • New units: travel, health, Ramadan & Eid, and telling stories

If there’s something you’d love to see, tell us. It helps us decide what comes first.
